Trinnex workspace in Huddle

Three pieces that work together: triage Fireflies meetings so they carry real titles, people and owners; load Trinnex files into a library; then ask Iris or Eli about any of it and get answers with sources. Everything is clickable. Nothing is saved.

All people, meetings and policies shown are invented examples, not your data.
